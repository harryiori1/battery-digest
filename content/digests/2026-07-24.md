## 03 Massachusetts to Pay EV Drivers for Their Battery Power
The state of Massachusetts is launching a program to incentivize EV owners to send electricity from their car batteries back to the grid when demand spikes. This program, known as vehicle-to-grid (V2G), aims to reduce strain on the grid during peak hours and promote the adoption of electric vehicles. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/23/massachusetts-ev-drivers-battery-power-v2g/), the program will provide EV owners with financial incentives for participating, with the goal of reducing greenhouse gas emissions and promoting a more sustainable energy system. This initiative is expected to have a positive impact on the environment and the EV industry as a whole.
