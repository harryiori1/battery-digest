## 02 Massachusetts to Pay EV Drivers for Their Battery Power
The state of Massachusetts is launching a new program to incentivize electric vehicle (EV) owners to send electricity from their car batteries back to the grid when demand spikes. This vehicle-to-grid (V2G) technology has the potential to stabilize the grid and provide additional revenue streams for EV owners. The program is part of a broader effort to promote the adoption of EVs and reduce greenhouse gas emissions. As reported by [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/23/massachusetts-ev-drivers-battery-power-v2g/), this initiative could pave the way for wider adoption of V2G technology in the US.
