## 03 Massachusetts to Pay EV Drivers for Their Battery Power
The state of Massachusetts is launching a program to pay electric vehicle (EV) drivers for sending electricity from their car batteries back to the grid when demand spikes. This move is seen as a way to incentivize the adoption of vehicle-to-grid (V2G) technology, which allows EVs to act as a source of energy storage and supply electricity back to the grid. The program is part of a broader effort to promote the use of EVs and reduce greenhouse gas emissions in the state. According to the announcement, eligible EV owners will be able to earn incentives for participating in the program, which could help to offset the cost of purchasing an EV. The program is seen as a significant step forward for the industry, as V2G technology has the potential to play a key role in the transition to a low-carbon economy.
