## 01 Europe Bets Big on Four-Day Iron-Air Battery
Ore Energy, a long-duration energy storage startup, has landed the largest iron-air battery deal in continental Europe, marking a significant milestone in the adoption of iron-air battery technology. The company has also raised Series A funding, which will be used to further develop its technology and expand its operations. Iron-air batteries have the potential to provide long-duration energy storage at a lower cost than traditional lithium-ion batteries, making them an attractive option for grid-scale energy storage applications. With this deal, Ore Energy is poised to play a major role in the European energy storage market, which is expected to grow significantly in the coming years. ## 02 China's Sodium-Ion Push Widens with First Electric Mining Truck Delivery
China has taken a significant step forward in its push for sodium-ion battery technology with the delivery of its first electric mining truck powered by sodium-ion batteries. The truck, which was developed by a Chinese company, marks a major milestone in the country's efforts to promote the adoption of sodium-ion batteries in various applications. Sodium-ion batteries have the potential to offer several advantages over traditional lithium-ion batteries, including lower costs and improved safety. With this delivery, China is demonstrating its commitment to developing and deploying sodium-ion battery technology, which could have significant implications for the global energy storage market.
