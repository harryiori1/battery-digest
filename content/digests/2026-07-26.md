## 01 Europe’s Next-Generation Submarines to Use Lithium-Ion Batteries
European defense contractor Saft is set to supply lithium-ion battery systems for the next generation of naval submarines. This upgrade replaces decades-old lead-acid technology with EV-style packs that promise higher energy density, faster charging, and superior underwater endurance. The use of lithium-ion batteries in submarines is expected to significantly improve their performance and capabilities. With the ability to store more energy and charge faster, these submarines will be able to stay underwater for longer periods, making them more effective in their operations. This development is a significant milestone in the adoption of lithium-ion batteries in the defense industry. [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/25/europes-next-generation-submarines-are-getting-a-lithium-ion-battery-upgrade/)

## 02 Sila Raises $300 Million to Scale Silicon Anode Production
Sila, a battery startup, has raised $300 million to scale its silicon anode production in the United States. The company claims that its silicon anodes can deliver 20% more range for electric vehicles. Sila is already working with major automakers such as Mercedes and Panasonic to integrate its technology into their vehicles. The use of silicon anodes has the potential to significantly improve the performance and efficiency of lithium-ion batteries, making electric vehicles more appealing to consumers. With this investment, Sila is well-positioned to become a leading supplier of silicon anodes to the automotive industry. [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/802397/sila-silicon-anode-lithium-ion/)

## 03 New Underground Energy Storage System Supports Wind and Solar Power
A new underground energy storage system has been developed to support wind and solar power. The system uses flywheels to store energy, which can be located largely underground, offering aesthetic benefits alongside the potential for lower costs, longer duration, and less reliance on overseas supply chains. This innovative approach to energy storage has the potential to play a significant role in the transition to renewable energy sources. By providing a reliable and efficient means of storing energy, this system can help to mitigate the intermittency of wind and solar power, making them more viable alternatives to traditional fossil fuels.
