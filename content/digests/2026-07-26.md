## 01 Europe’s Next-Generation Submarines to Use Lithium-Ion Batteries
European defense contractor Saft is set to supply lithium-ion battery systems for the next generation of naval submarines. This upgrade replaces decades-old lead-acid technology with EV-style packs that promise higher energy density, faster charging, and superior underwater endurance. The use of lithium-ion batteries in submarines is expected to significantly improve their performance and capabilities. With higher energy density, the submarines will be able to stay underwater for longer periods, and the faster charging times will enable them to quickly recharge at naval bases. This development is a significant milestone in the adoption of lithium-ion battery technology in the defense sector. ## 02 Sila Raises $300 Million to Scale Silicon Anode Production
Sila, a battery startup, has raised $300 million to scale its silicon anode production in the US. The company's silicon anode technology promises to deliver 20% more range for electric vehicles. Sila is already working with major automakers, including Mercedes and Panasonic, to integrate its technology into their EV batteries. The funding will enable Sila to increase its production capacity and meet the growing demand for its silicon anode technology. This development is significant for the EV industry, as it could lead to the production of more efficient and longer-range electric vehicles. ## 03 Sila’s Silicon Anode to Deliver 20% More EV Range
Sila's silicon anode technology has the potential to revolutionize the EV industry by delivering 20% more range for electric vehicles. The company's technology replaces the traditional graphite anode with a silicon-based material, which has a higher energy density. This results in a significant increase in the battery's overall energy storage capacity, leading to longer ranges for EVs. Sila is already working with major automakers to integrate its technology into their EV batteries, and the company's scaling of production is expected to have a significant impact on the industry. With the growing demand for EVs, Sila's technology is poised to play a crucial role in the development of more efficient and longer-range electric vehicles.
