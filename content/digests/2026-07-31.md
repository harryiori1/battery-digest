## 01 1 GWh Battery Under Construction in Ohio
A massive 1 GWh battery is currently under construction outside Columbus, Ohio, as reported by [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/30/pjm-grid-largest-battery-is-now-under-construction-in-ohio/). This project aims to meet the rapidly increasing electricity demand in the area, driven by the growth of data centers and industrial development. The construction of this large-scale battery highlights the importance of energy storage in supporting the grid and ensuring a stable power supply. With a capacity of 1 GWh, this battery will be the largest in the PJM grid, demonstrating the increasing role of energy storage in the energy landscape.

## 02 Mirattery Deepens Securitization Push with New REITs
Mirattery, the battery operator of Chinese electric vehicle manufacturer Nio, is expanding its securitization efforts with the introduction of new real estate investment trusts (REITs), as reported by [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/07/31/mirattery-securitization-new-reits/). This move is part of Mirattery's strategy to diversify its financing channels and reduce its reliance on traditional funding methods. By securitizing its assets, Mirattery can unlock new sources of capital and improve its financial flexibility, ultimately supporting the growth of its battery operations.

## 03 Shanghai Wash Invests 100 Million Yuan in Solid-State Battery Project
Keyuan Solid Energy, a subsidiary of Shanghai Wash, plans to invest 100 million yuan (approximately $14.5 million USD) in a solid-state battery project, as reported by [36氪](https://36kr.com/newsflashes/3919415518703238?f=rss). The project aims to develop a 0.5GWh solid-liquid hybrid battery cell and a 500-ton per year solid-state electrolyte powder production line. This investment demonstrates Shanghai Wash's commitment to advancing solid-state battery technology, which has the potential to significantly improve the performance, safety, and efficiency of electric vehicle batteries. With this project, Keyuan Solid Energy is poised to play a key role in the development of next-generation battery technologies.
