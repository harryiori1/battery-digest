## 1 GWh Battery Under Construction in Ohio
A massive 1 GWh battery is currently under construction in Ohio, outside of Columbus. This project aims to meet the rapidly increasing electricity demand in the area, driven by the growth of data centers and industrial development. The battery will be the largest in the PJM grid, which serves several states in the northeastern United States. This development highlights the crucial role that energy storage systems play in supporting the grid and ensuring a stable supply of electricity. As the demand for renewable energy sources continues to rise, the need for large-scale energy storage solutions like this battery will only continue to grow. ## Mirattery Deepens Securitization Push with New REITs
Mirattery, the battery operator owned by Nio, has announced its plans to deepen its securitization push with the introduction of new real estate investment trusts (REITs). This move is part of the company's efforts to expand its financial offerings and attract more investors. By securitizing its battery assets, Mirattery aims to unlock new funding channels and reduce its financing costs. This development is significant for the EV battery supply chain, as it demonstrates the growing importance of financial innovation in supporting the growth of the industry. [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/07/31/mirattery-securitization-new-reits/)

## Antora Energy Raises $550 Million for Thermal Energy Storage
Antora Energy, a US-based startup, has secured $550 million in funding to scale up its thermal energy storage technology. The company's 'hot blocks' system uses carbon blocks to store thermal energy, which can then be used to generate electricity. This technology has the potential to replace fossil fuels and support the transition to renewable energy sources. With this new funding, Antora Energy plans to accelerate the development and deployment of its technology, with the goal of reducing greenhouse gas emissions and promoting a more sustainable energy system.
