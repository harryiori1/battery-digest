## 01 Tesla Megapack Battery Storage Project Goes Live in Texas
A 500 MWh Tesla Megapack battery storage project has been launched in Texas to support the grid. The project, located at Ørsted’s new 250 MW/500 MWh Old 300 battery storage facility, aims to provide stability and reliability to the Texas grid. This is a significant development in the battery storage sector, as it demonstrates the growing importance of energy storage in supporting renewable energy sources and ensuring grid stability. The project's capacity of 500 MWh is notable, as it can power approximately 100,000 homes for several hours. Tesla's Megapack batteries are designed to provide long-duration energy storage, making them well-suited for grid-scale applications. ## 03 U.S. Battery Storage Capacity Sees 70% Average Annual Growth Over Last Three Years
The United States has seen significant growth in utility-scale battery storage capacity, with an average annual growth rate of 70% over the last three years. This growth is driven by the increasing demand for renewable energy sources and the need for energy storage to support grid stability. The total operational battery storage capacity in the United States has reached 43.6 GW, with an additional 8.3 GW added in the first six months of 2026. This growth is expected to continue, as more states and companies invest in battery storage technology. The benefits of battery storage are numerous, including improved grid resilience, reduced greenhouse gas emissions, and increased renewable energy penetration.
