## 01 GM Exits $3.5B Indiana Battery Plant as Samsung SDI Takes Full Control
Samsung SDI is taking full control of its Indiana battery plant by buying out General Motors' 49.99% stake in their joint venture. This move ends GM's ownership in the $3.5 billion factory and turns the New Carlisle, Indiana plant into Samsung SDI's first wholly owned battery factory in North America. The acquisition is a significant development in the EV battery supply chain, as Samsung SDI aims to expand its presence in the North American market. With this move, Samsung SDI will have complete control over the plant's operations, allowing it to optimize production and meet the growing demand for EV batteries. ## 02 BYD Launches 2027 Seal 06 with 630 km Range, Second-Gen Blade Battery
BYD has launched its new Seal 06 model, featuring a second-generation Blade Battery that provides a range of 630 km. The new battery technology is a significant upgrade, offering improved performance and efficiency. The Seal 06 is available as both a pure electric model and a plug-in hybrid, catering to a wide range of customers. The introduction of the second-gen Blade Battery is a notable development in the EV industry, as it demonstrates BYD's commitment to advancing battery technology and providing customers with high-performance vehicles. [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/08/11/byd-launches-2027-seal-06/)

## 03 Oil Giant Sinopec Replaces Gas Pumps with BYD's 1,500 kW EV Chargers
Sinopec, China's biggest fuel retailer, has converted a Shanghai gas station into an EV-only charging hub, installing BYD's 1,500 kW flash chargers. The move marks a significant shift towards electrification, as Sinopec replaces traditional gas pumps with high-power EV chargers. The charging hub will provide fast and convenient charging services to EV owners, supporting the growth of the EV market in China. The partnership between Sinopec and BYD demonstrates the increasing collaboration between oil companies and EV manufacturers, as they work together to build a more sustainable transportation infrastructure. [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/08/11/sinopec-byd-gas-station-1500-kw-flash-charging/)
