## 01 McLaren P1 Gets 3X Battery Capacity Upgrade
The McLaren P1, a plug-in hybrid hypercar that debuted over a decade ago, is receiving a significant battery upgrade. The new battery technology will increase the car's capacity by three times, effectively modernizing the vehicle and keeping it competitive in the current market. This upgrade is a testament to the rapid advancements being made in battery technology and its potential to breathe new life into older models. According to [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/804474/mclaren-p1-battery-upgrade/), the exact specifications of the new battery have not been released, but the upgrade is expected to significantly improve the car's performance and range.

## 02 GM Sells Stake in $3.5B Indiana Battery Factory to Samsung SDI
General Motors has sold its stake in a $3.5 billion battery factory in Indiana to Samsung SDI, marking the end of their joint venture. Despite this, Samsung SDI plans to continue with the construction of the factory, which is expected to produce batteries for electric vehicles. This development highlights the growing importance of battery manufacturing in the automotive industry and the increasing competition among companies to establish themselves as leaders in this field. As reported by [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/804446/gm-sells-indiana-battery-factory-stake-samsung-sdi/), the factory's production capacity and exact timeline have not been disclosed.

## 03 2027 BYD Seal 06 Features Advanced Lidar Technology
The 2027 BYD Seal 06, a new model from the Chinese automaker, features advanced lidar technology integrated into the roof and windshield of the vehicle. The car also boasts BYD's God's Eye B ADAS system, which is an upgrade from the previous model. While the article from [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/08/11/2027-byd-seal-06-comes-to-lidar/) focuses on the lidar technology and ADAS system, it does not provide details on the battery specifications of the new model. However, the inclusion of advanced technology such as lidar suggests that the 2027 BYD Seal 06 is likely to have a competitive battery system as well.
