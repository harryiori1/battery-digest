## 01 GM Exits $3.5B Indiana Battery Plant as Samsung SDI Takes Full Control
In a significant development for the EV battery industry, Samsung SDI has acquired General Motors' 49.99% stake in their joint venture, taking full control of the $3.5 billion New Carlisle, Indiana battery plant. This move marks Samsung SDI's first wholly owned battery factory in North America, demonstrating the company's commitment to expanding its presence in the region. The plant, which has a production capacity of 10 GWh, will continue to supply batteries to GM for use in their electric vehicles. This deal highlights the growing importance of battery manufacturing in the EV supply chain and the increasing competition among battery makers to establish a strong presence in key markets. ## 02 BYD Launches 2027 Seal 06 with 630 km Range, Second-Gen Blade Battery
BYD has unveiled its latest model, the 2027 Seal 06, featuring an impressive range of 630 km and the company's second-generation Blade Battery technology. The new battery technology is expected to provide improved safety, efficiency, and performance. The Seal 06 is available in both pure electric and plug-in hybrid variants, catering to a wide range of customer needs. This launch demonstrates BYD's ongoing efforts to enhance its EV offerings and stay competitive in the rapidly evolving market. With the introduction of the second-gen Blade Battery, BYD aims to further establish itself as a leader in the EV battery sector. [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/08/11/byd-launches-2027-seal-06/)

## 03 Sinopec Replaces Gas Pumps with BYD's 1,500 kW EV Chargers
In a significant move towards electrification, Sinopec, China's largest fuel retailer, has converted one of its gas stations in Shanghai into an EV-only charging hub. The station now features BYD's high-power 1,500 kW flash chargers, capable of charging vehicles at an incredibly fast rate. This development marks a notable shift in the industry, as traditional fuel retailers begin to adapt to the growing demand for electric vehicles. The partnership between Sinopec and BYD highlights the increasing collaboration between companies from different sectors to drive the adoption of EVs and support the development of charging infrastructure.
