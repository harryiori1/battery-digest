## 01 Xiaomi Launches Dragon Armor Battery System
Xiaomi has officially launched its Dragon Armor Battery System, a comprehensive solution for electric vehicles. This system covers product definition, design, development, and quality control of battery packs, with Xiaomi taking the lead in these areas. The company will also participate in the design and development of battery cells. This move marks a significant step for Xiaomi in the electric vehicle industry, as it seeks to establish itself as a major player in the market. The Dragon Armor Battery System is expected to provide a competitive edge for Xiaomi's electric vehicles, with a focus on safety, performance, and efficiency. ## 02 Dongfeng Unveils 1.5 MW EV Charger
Dongfeng has unveiled a high-power EV charger with a capacity of 1.5 MW, rivaling BYD's flash charger. The charger, developed in-house, can support a wide range of battery voltages, peaking at 1,000V. This solution is designed to cater to the growing demand for fast and efficient charging infrastructure, enabling electric vehicles to charge quickly and conveniently. The launch of this charger demonstrates Dongfeng's commitment to advancing electric vehicle technology and providing innovative solutions for the industry.
