## 02 CCC Certification for Charging Piles to be Mandatory Starting August 1
The Chinese government has implemented mandatory CCC certification for electric vehicle charging equipment, effective August 1. This move aims to ensure the safety and quality of charging piles, which are critical infrastructure for the growing number of electric vehicles on the road. The certification process involves rigorous testing and inspection of charging equipment to guarantee compliance with safety standards. This development is expected to have a significant impact on the EV industry, as it will promote the use of safe and reliable charging equipment. [36氪](https://36kr.com/newsflashes/3920582349974916)
