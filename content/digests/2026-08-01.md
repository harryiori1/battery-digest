## 02 Janus Battery Swap Trucks Show the Way for Electric Truck Swap Infrastructure
Janus Electric, an Australian company, is developing battery swap trucks, providing a potential solution for electric truck swap infrastructure. This development follows CATL's joint venture to introduce electric truck swap infrastructure to Europe. The battery swap technology allows for faster and more efficient charging of electric trucks, making it an attractive solution for the logistics and transportation industry. Janus Electric's battery swap trucks can be used for a variety of applications, including last-mile delivery, courier services, and waste management. The company's innovative approach to electric truck swap infrastructure has the potential to disrupt the traditional charging model and provide a more convenient and cost-effective solution for electric truck owners. ## 03 Lower Saxony’s Hydrogen Trains Face Battery-Electric Multiple Units as a Better Alternative
Lower Saxony's hydrogen trains may be replaced by battery-electric multiple units, which are considered a better technology. The state is comparing the costs of extending the life of the hydrogen system with buying new battery-electric units. The decision to replace the hydrogen trains with battery-electric multiple units is driven by the higher efficiency and lower operating costs of the latter. Battery-electric multiple units are also more environmentally friendly, as they produce zero emissions and require less energy to operate. The replacement of hydrogen trains with battery-electric multiple units is a significant development in the transportation sector, as it highlights the growing trend towards electrification and the adoption of more sustainable technologies.
