## 02 CCC Certification for Charging Piles to be Mandatory Starting August 1
The Chinese government has announced that CCC certification for electric vehicle charging piles will be mandatory, effective August 1. This move aims to ensure the quality and safety of charging piles, which are a critical component of the electric vehicle ecosystem. The CCC certification process involves testing and evaluation of charging piles to ensure they meet certain safety and performance standards. This includes tests for electrical safety, fire resistance, and other key performance indicators. By making CCC certification mandatory, the government is taking a significant step towards promoting the adoption of electric vehicles and ensuring the safety of users.
