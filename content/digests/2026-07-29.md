## 01 SK On and Factorial Energy Partner to Scale Solid-State Battery Tech
SK On and Factorial Energy have announced a new alliance aimed at bringing solid-state batteries to the masses. The partnership will focus on scaling the "breakthrough" technology, which promises to enhance the performance, safety, and efficiency of electric vehicle (EV) batteries. Solid-state batteries replace the liquid electrolyte in traditional lithium-ion batteries with a solid material, reducing the risk of fires and improving charging speeds. The collaboration between SK On and Factorial Energy is expected to accelerate the development and commercialization of solid-state batteries, potentially revolutionizing the EV industry. ## 02 FAW Hongqi Develops Ultra-Fast Charging Battery with 10-70% Charge in 3 Minutes 41 Seconds
FAW Hongqi has developed an ultra-fast charging battery that can charge from 10% to 70% in just 3 minutes and 41 seconds. This achievement demonstrates significant advancements in battery technology, enabling rapid charging capabilities that outpace existing solutions. The new battery is expected to enhance the driving experience for EV owners, providing them with greater flexibility and convenience. As the demand for EVs continues to grow, innovations like FAW Hongqi's ultra-fast charging battery will play a crucial role in shaping the future of the industry. ## 03 BYD Reaches 100,000 EVs Produced in Brazil, Expanding Global Presence
BYD has reached a significant milestone, producing 100,000 electric vehicles (EVs) in Brazil.
