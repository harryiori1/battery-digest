## 01 SK On and Factorial Energy Partner to Scale Solid-State Batteries
SK On and Factorial Energy have announced a new alliance aimed at bringing solid-state battery technology to the masses. The partnership will focus on developing and commercializing solid-state batteries, which have the potential to significantly improve the range and efficiency of electric vehicles. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/29/solid-state-ev-battery-leaders-team-up/), the two companies will work together to overcome the technical and manufacturing challenges associated with solid-state batteries. This partnership is a significant development in the battery industry, as it brings together two leading companies in the field of solid-state battery technology.

## 02 FAW Hongqi Develops Ultra-Fast Charging Battery
FAW Hongqi has developed an ultra-fast charging battery that can charge from 10% to 70% in just 3 minutes and 41 seconds. This achievement is a significant breakthrough in battery technology, as it has the potential to greatly improve the convenience and practicality of electric vehicles. According to [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/07/28/faw-hongqi-battery-charges-10-70-3-mins-41-secs/), the battery's fast charging capability is due to its advanced chemistry and design. This development is a major milestone in the development of ultra-fast charging technology, which is expected to play a key role in the widespread adoption of electric vehicles.

## 03 BYD Reaches 100,000 EVs Produced in Brazil
BYD has reached a significant milestone in its Brazilian production facility, with over 100,000 electric vehicles produced to date. This achievement is a testament to the company's commitment to expanding its presence in the global EV market. According to [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/07/27/byd-reaches-100000-evs-produced-in-brazil/), the Brazilian facility is BYD's largest outside of Asia, and it has played a key role in the company's efforts to increase its global production capacity. This milestone is a significant development in the EV industry, as it highlights the growing importance of international production facilities in meeting the increasing demand for electric vehicles.
