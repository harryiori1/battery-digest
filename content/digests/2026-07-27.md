## 02 ESS & Juniper Energy Sign Agreement for 500 MWh+ of Sodium-Ion Energy Storage
ESS Tech, Inc. has announced a partnership with Juniper Energy LLC to deploy over 500 MWh of sodium-ion energy storage. The agreement starts with an 80 MWh project in California, establishing a framework for long-term collaboration. This significant deployment of sodium-ion energy storage solutions marks a notable advancement in the field of renewable energy. Sodium-ion batteries offer a promising alternative to traditional lithium-ion batteries, with potential cost and sustainability benefits. The partnership between ESS and Juniper Energy is expected to drive the adoption of sodium-ion energy storage solutions in the market. [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/07/27/ess-juniper-energy-sign-agreement-for-500-mwh-of-sodium-ion-energy-storage-deployments/)
