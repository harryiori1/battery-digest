## 01 ESS & Juniper Energy Sign Agreement for 500 MWh+ of Sodium-Ion Energy Storage Deployments
ESS Tech, Inc and Juniper Energy LLC have signed a Letter of Intent for the deployment of over 500 MWh of sodium-ion energy storage solutions. The partnership begins with a planned 80 MWh project in California and establishes a framework for long-term collaboration. This agreement represents a significant step forward for the adoption of sodium-ion batteries in the energy storage market. Sodium-ion batteries offer a potentially more cost-effective and sustainable alternative to traditional lithium-ion batteries. The partnership between ESS and Juniper Energy is expected to drive the development and deployment of sodium-ion energy storage solutions, supporting the growth of renewable energy sources. ## 02 Hawaiian Electric Seeks to Expand Renewables, Energy Storage on Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi Island, and Maui
Hawaiian Electric Company has submitted its Integrated Grid Planning report, seeking to expand renewables and energy storage on several islands. The plan represents significant progress toward achieving 100% renewable energy and includes the addition of 500 MW of firm generation on O‘ahu. The company's efforts to expand energy storage and renewables will support the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions and promote a more sustainable energy mix. The integration of energy storage solutions will play a crucial role in ensuring a stable and reliable energy supply as the company transitions to renewable energy sources. ## 03 BYD Wins Hong Kong Land Bid for HK$12 Million to Build Electric Vehicle Charging Station
BYD has won a land bid in Hong Kong for HK$12 million to build an electric vehicle charging station, with a lease period of 12 years. The land, located in Kowloon, will be used to construct a charging station with an area of approximately 1022.1 square meters. This development supports the growth of electric vehicle adoption in Hong Kong and highlights BYD's commitment to expanding its presence in the region. The construction of the charging station will help address the increasing demand for electric vehicle charging infrastructure, promoting the transition to more sustainable transportation options.
