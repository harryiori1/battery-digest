## 01 CATL Aviation Battery Clears Key Safety Test
CATL, a leading battery manufacturer, has announced that its aviation battery has cleared a key safety test, paving the way for its use in electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft. This development is significant for the aviation industry, as it brings the prospect of electrified flight closer to reality. The successful safety validation of CATL's aviation battery is a crucial step towards the commercialization of eVTOLs, which are expected to revolutionize urban air mobility. With this achievement, CATL is poised to play a major role in the electrification of the aviation sector. [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/08/09/catl-aviation-battery-clears-key-safety-test/)

## 02 1 GWh Battery Under Construction in Ohio
A massive 1 GWh battery is currently under construction outside Columbus, Ohio, marking a significant development in the state's energy storage landscape. The project aims to support the growing electricity demand from data centers and industrial development in the region. This large-scale battery storage system will help stabilize the grid, provide backup power during outages, and enable greater integration of renewable energy sources. The construction of this battery highlights the increasing importance of energy storage in meeting the rising electricity demands of industrial and commercial sectors. ## 03 Ningde Era Lithium Mine Production Status Unclear
Rumors have been circulating about the resumption of production at Ningde Era's lithium mine. However, local officials have responded to these rumors, confirming that the mine remains suspended and is still undergoing maintenance and repairs. The status of the mine's production is crucial for the global lithium supply chain, as Ningde Era is a significant player in the industry. Any updates on the mine's operational status could have implications for the pricing and availability of lithium, a key material in battery production.
