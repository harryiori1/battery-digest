## 01 World's First Sodium-Ion Electric Haul Truck Deployed in China
Tonly, a Chinese company, has deployed the world's first sodium-ion electric haul truck in a mining operation. The truck is equipped with advanced sodium-ion batteries, which provide a reliable and efficient source of power. This deployment marks a significant milestone in the adoption of sodium-ion batteries in the mining industry. The use of sodium-ion batteries is expected to reduce the environmental impact of mining operations and improve the overall efficiency of the process. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/08/06/worlds-first-sodium-ion-electric-haul-truck-gets-to-work-in-china/), the truck is capable of hauling heavy ore, demonstrating the potential of sodium-ion batteries in heavy-duty applications.

## 02 CALB Responds to 'Banana Battery' Fallout with Quality Overhaul
CALB, a Chinese battery manufacturer, has announced a quality overhaul in response to the "banana battery" issue. The issue, which refers to the deformation of battery cells, has raised concerns about the reliability and performance of CALB's batteries. According to [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/08/06/calb-banana-battery-quality-overhaul/), CALB is taking steps to improve the quality of its batteries, including the implementation of new manufacturing processes and quality control measures. This move is expected to improve the overall performance and reliability of CALB's batteries, addressing concerns raised by the "banana battery" issue.

## 03 Electric Mining Truck Uses Sodium-Ion Batteries, a New Era of Electrification
Hina Battery and Shaanxi Tonly Heavy Industries have collaborated on the development of an electric mining truck that uses sodium-ion batteries. This truck marks a significant step in the adoption of sodium-ion batteries in the mining industry, demonstrating the potential of this technology in heavy-duty applications. According to [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/08/05/electric-mining-truck-uses-sodium-ion-batteries/), the use of sodium-ion batteries in electric mining trucks is expected to reduce the environmental impact of mining operations and improve the overall efficiency of the process. This development is a significant milestone in the transition to a more sustainable and efficient mining industry.
