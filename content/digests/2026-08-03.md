## 01 Base Power Raises $1B for Giant Home Battery
Base Power has unveiled Core, a supersized 39.2-kWh home battery that it says can keep a house running for up to 36 hours and be installed in under an hour. The company has raised $1 billion to roll out the new product, which is designed to provide a reliable source of backup power for homes. The Core battery is expected to be a game-changer in the energy storage market, with its large capacity and fast installation time making it an attractive option for homeowners. ## 02 BYD Denza Opens Z9S Pre-Orders with 1,100-km Range
BYD Denza has opened pre-orders for the Z9S, a new electric vehicle with a range of 1,100 km. The vehicle is priced from $47,100 and is expected to be a popular option for those looking for a long-range electric vehicle. The Z9S is the latest addition to BYD's lineup of electric vehicles, which have been gaining popularity in recent years. [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/08/03/byd-denza-opens-z9s-pre-orders/)

## 03 Ganfeng Lithium Expects to Turn Losses into Profits in First Half
Ganfeng Lithium expects to turn losses into profits in the first half of the year, with a maximum net profit of 4.6 billion yuan. The company's net profit is expected to increase significantly due to the growing demand for lithium-ion batteries. Ganfeng Lithium is one of the largest lithium producers in the world and is expected to benefit from the increasing demand for electric vehicles and energy storage systems.
