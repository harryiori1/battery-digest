## 01 ProLogium Breaks Ground on Dunkirk Gigafactory in France
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery company, has announced the groundbreaking of its Dunkirk gigafactory in France. This significant investment marks a major milestone in the company's plans to expand its production capacity and meet the growing demand for solid-state batteries in the European market. The Dunkirk gigafactory is expected to create hundreds of jobs and contribute to the local economy. With this new facility, ProLogium aims to accelerate the adoption of solid-state batteries in the electric vehicle industry, enabling faster charging, longer ranges, and improved safety.

## 02 Tianzhikefang Completes Pre-A Round Financing for Sulfide Solid-State Electrolyte Materials
Tianzhikefang, a Chinese company specializing in sulfide solid-state electrolyte materials, has completed its pre-A round financing. The funding will be used to support the development of high-performance solid-state electrolyte materials, which are crucial for the production of solid-state batteries. Tianzhikefang's technology has shown promising results, with its sulfide solid-state electrolyte materials demonstrating excellent air stability and high ionic conductivity. This investment is expected to drive the company's growth and contribute to the advancement of solid-state battery technology.

## 03 Blue Solutions Demonstrates 70% More Autonomy with Breakthrough GEN4 Lithium-Metal Batteries
Blue Solutions, a French company, has announced a significant breakthrough in its GEN4 lithium-metal battery technology. The company has demonstrated a 70% increase in autonomy, making its batteries more competitive in the electric vehicle market. Blue Solutions' GEN4 batteries utilize a solid-state design, which provides improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds. This achievement is a major step forward for the company and highlights the potential of solid-state battery technology to revolutionize the electric vehicle industry.
