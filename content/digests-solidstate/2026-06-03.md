## 01 ProLogium Files for IPO as Solid-State Battery Race Heats Up
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery developer, has filed for an initial public offering (IPO) through a $3.8 billion SPAC deal, marking a significant milestone in the solid-state battery industry. As the solid-state battery market continues to heat up, ProLogium's IPO is seen as a major step forward for the industry, with many experts predicting that solid-state batteries will eventually replace traditional lithium-ion batteries in electric vehicles and other applications. With its IPO, ProLogium is well-positioned to play a leading role in this transition.

## 02 New Solid-State Battery Tech Shrugs Off Extreme Temperatures
Recent tests have shown that new solid-state battery technology can withstand temperatures as low as -40°C and as high as 55°C, a significant improvement over traditional lithium-ion batteries. This breakthrough has major implications for the development of solid-state batteries, which are seen as a key component in the transition to electric vehicles and renewable energy. The new technology, which uses a solid electrolyte instead of a liquid one, is more stable and less prone to overheating, making it safer and more reliable than traditional batteries. As the solid-state battery industry continues to evolve, this new technology is expected to play a major role in its development.
