## 01 ProLogium and OPmobility Partner to Develop Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Modules
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery company, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with OPmobility to develop next-generation solid-state battery modules for mobility applications. This partnership aims to accelerate the development and commercialization of solid-state battery technology, which is expected to play a crucial role in the electrification of transportation. According to the MoU, ProLogium will develop and supply the solid-state battery cells, while OPmobility will design and manufacture the battery modules. The partnership will focus on developing high-performance, safe, and reliable battery modules that meet the demanding requirements of the automotive industry. This collaboration is a significant step forward for the solid-state battery industry, as it brings together two companies with complementary expertise and resources. With this partnership, ProLogium and OPmobility are poised to make a significant impact on the development of next-generation electric vehicles.
