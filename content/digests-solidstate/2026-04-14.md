## 02 Zwitterions Are The Key To New Solid-State Batteries
Scientists at Oak Ridge National Laboratory have created a new electrolyte using zwitterions, which could lead to the development of more efficient and safer solid-state batteries. As reported by [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/04/13/zwitterions-are-the-key-to-new-solid-state-batteries/), the new electrolyte has shown promising results, with improved ionic conductivity and stability. Zwitterions are molecules that have both positive and negative charges, which makes them ideal for use in solid-state batteries. This breakthrough could lead to the development of more efficient and longer-lasting batteries, enabling the widespread adoption of electric vehicles.
