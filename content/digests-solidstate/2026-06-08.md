## 01 ProLogium to List on Nasdaq through Merger
ProLogium, a next-generation solid-state battery developer, has announced plans to list on the Nasdaq through a merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp. This move is expected to provide ProLogium with the necessary funding to further develop and commercialize its solid-state battery technology. With over 10 years of proven commercialization experience, ProLogium is well-positioned to become a leading player in the solid-state battery market. As stated on their [website](https://prologium.com/), ProLogium's solid-state battery technology has the potential to revolutionize the energy storage industry.

## 02 Argonne Scientists Discover How to Boost Solid-State Battery Energy Density and Longevity
Researchers at Argonne National Laboratory have made a breakthrough in solid-state battery technology, discovering a way to increase energy density and longevity. This discovery has the potential to significantly improve the performance of solid-state batteries, making them more competitive with traditional lithium-ion batteries. According to the [Argonne National Laboratory website](https://anl.gov/), the researchers used advanced materials and manufacturing techniques to achieve this breakthrough.

## 03 ProLogium’s Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Integrated into Nakayama Iron Works’ Electric Self-Propelled Soil Improvement Machine
ProLogium's solid-state battery technology has been integrated into Nakayama Iron Works' NE100MG electric self-propelled soil improvement machine, marking a significant milestone in the commercialization of solid-state batteries. This integration demonstrates the potential of solid-state batteries to be used in a wide range of applications, from electric vehicles to industrial equipment. As reported on [ProLogium's website](https://prologium.com/), this partnership is expected to drive the adoption of solid-state batteries in the industrial sector.
