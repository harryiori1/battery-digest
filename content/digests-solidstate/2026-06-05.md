## 01 QuantumScape and Solid Power Progress in Solid-State Battery Development
QuantumScape and Solid Power, two leading companies in the solid-state battery industry, have recently made significant progress in their development efforts. QuantumScape has achieved a major milestone with its eagle line production, while Solid Power has reported progress in its EV battery milestones, despite a decline in Q1 revenue. These advancements demonstrate the ongoing efforts to commercialize solid-state batteries, which promise to revolutionize the electric vehicle industry with improved safety, efficiency, and range. As [Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4572473-quantumscape-battery-bet-risky-but-upside-just-got-bigger) notes, QuantumScape's progress is a significant step towards making solid-state batteries a reality.

## 02 Nissan Collaborates with Partners on Sulphur-Based Solid-State Battery Research
Nissan has partnered with several companies to develop sulphur-based solid-state batteries, which have the potential to be more efficient and sustainable than traditional lithium-ion batteries. This collaboration, as reported by [electrive.com](https://electrive.com/2023/06/05/nissan-collaborates-with-partners-on-sulphur-based-solid-state-battery-research/), aims to accelerate the development of solid-state batteries and reduce the industry's reliance on lithium. The use of sulphur-based materials could also lead to cost savings and improved performance.

## 03 Solid Power Reports Q1 Revenue Fall, but EV Battery Milestones Keep Growth Story Alive
Solid Power, a leading solid-state battery company, has reported a decline in Q1 revenue, but the company's progress in EV battery development and partnerships has kept its growth story alive.
