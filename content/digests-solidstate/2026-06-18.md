## 01 ProLogium Partners with Elysian Aircraft to Explore Solid-State Battery Applications in Aerospace
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery manufacturer, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Elysian Aircraft to explore the development of next-generation solid-state battery modules for aerospace applications. This partnership aims to advance the vision of zero-emission aviation and future mobility. ProLogium's solid-state battery technology has the potential to revolutionize the aerospace industry by providing a safer, more efficient, and more sustainable energy storage solution. With this partnership, ProLogium and Elysian Aircraft will work together to develop and integrate solid-state battery modules into aircraft, enabling the creation of more efficient and environmentally friendly aircraft.

## 02 Blue Solutions Launches Joint Safety Program with AVL for Next-Generation Solid-State Cell Technology
Blue Solutions, a pioneer in solid-state battery technology, has launched a joint safety program with AVL, a leading automotive consulting firm, to prepare for the scale-up of next-generation solid-state cell technology. This program focuses on ensuring the safety and performance of solid-state batteries, which is critical for their adoption in the automotive and aerospace industries. By partnering with AVL, Blue Solutions aims to develop a comprehensive safety framework for solid-state batteries, addressing key challenges such as thermal management, electrical safety, and mechanical integrity. This collaboration will enable Blue Solutions to accelerate the development and commercialization of its solid-state battery technology.

## 03 QuantumScape Stock Surges Following Latest Honda Pact and Solid-State Battery Developments
QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery manufacturer, has announced a new pact with Honda, which has sent its stock soaring.
