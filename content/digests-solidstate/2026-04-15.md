## 01 Greater Bay Technology Aims to Launch Mass-Produced All-Solid-State Battery This Year
Greater Bay Technology has announced a breakthrough in solid-state battery technology, aiming to launch the world's first mass-producible all-solid-state battery this year. This development is significant for the commercialization of solid-state batteries, which are expected to replace traditional lithium-ion batteries in the near future. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/04/15/solid-state-ev-batteries-coming-sooner-than-expected/), Greater Bay Technology's achievement brings the industry closer to widespread adoption of solid-state batteries. The company's progress is a testament to the rapid advancements being made in solid-state battery technology, with potential implications for the electric vehicle (EV) industry and beyond.

## 02 ProLogium Breaks Ground on Dunkirk Gigafactory in France
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery manufacturer, has broken ground on its new gigafactory in Dunkirk, France. As reported on [ProLogium's website](https://prologium.com/prologium-breaks-ground-on-dunkirk-gigafactory-in-france/), this new facility will significantly boost the company's solid-state battery production capacity. The gigafactory is expected to play a crucial role in meeting the growing demand for solid-state batteries in the EV and other industries. ProLogium's expansion into France is a strategic move, aiming to capitalize on the region's skilled workforce and favorable business environment.
