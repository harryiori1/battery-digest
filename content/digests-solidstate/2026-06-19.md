## 01 Stellantis and Factorial Integrate Advanced Solid-State Battery into Development Vehicle
Stellantis and Factorial have successfully integrated an advanced solid-state battery into a Stellantis development vehicle, marking a significant milestone in the development of solid-state battery technology. The integrated battery has begun road testing, which will help to validate its performance, safety, and durability. This collaboration between Stellantis and Factorial demonstrates the progress being made in the development of solid-state batteries for automotive applications. According to [media.stellantis.com](https://media.stellantis.com), the road testing will provide valuable insights into the battery's behavior in real-world driving conditions.
