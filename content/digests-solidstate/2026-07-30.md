## 01 Electrical Imbalances at Grain Boundaries Help Explain Solid-State Battery Failure
Researchers have made a significant breakthrough in understanding the failure of solid-state batteries, citing electrical imbalances at grain boundaries as a key factor. According to a recent study published on [Phys.org](https://phys.org/), the electrical imbalances at grain boundaries can lead to a reduction in the overall performance and lifespan of solid-state batteries. This discovery could have significant implications for the development of more efficient and reliable solid-state batteries. The study suggests that by understanding and addressing these electrical imbalances, manufacturers can improve the overall quality and performance of solid-state batteries, paving the way for their widespread adoption in electric vehicles and other applications.
