## 01 ProLogium Advances Solid-State Battery Technology with Multiple Partnerships
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery developer, has signed multiple partnerships to advance the development of next-generation solid-state battery applications. The company has signed MoUs with Elysian Aircraft BV and OPmobility to explore the use of solid-state batteries in aerospace and mobility applications. These partnerships demonstrate ProLogium's commitment to advancing solid-state battery technology and its potential to revolutionize various industries. According to [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/), the partnerships will enable the development of more efficient, safer, and sustainable battery solutions.

## 02 ProLogium to List on Nasdaq through Merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp
ProLogium, a next-generation solid-state battery developer, has announced its plans to list on the Nasdaq through a merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp. This merger will provide ProLogium with the necessary funding to further develop its solid-state battery technology and expand its operations. As stated on [ProLogium's website](https://prologium.com/), the company has over 10 years of proven commercialization experience and has developed a range of solid-state battery products.

## 03 ProLogium’s Solid-State Battery Integrated into Nakayama Iron Works’ Electric Machine
ProLogium’s next-generation solid-state battery has been integrated into Nakayama Iron Works’ NE100MG electric self-propelled soil improvement machine. This integration demonstrates the potential of solid-state batteries to be used in various industrial applications, providing a more efficient and sustainable solution. As reported by [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/), the NE100MG machine is designed to improve soil quality and reduce environmental impact, and the use of ProLogium’s solid-state battery will further enhance its performance and sustainability.
