## 01 Tianzhikefeng Completes Pre-A Round Financing for Solid-State Electrolyte Materials
Tianzhikefeng, a Chinese company specializing in sulfide solid-state electrolyte materials, has completed its pre-A round financing, securing tens of millions of yuan in funding. The investment, led by Yuanyuoyuan, will be used to develop the company's products and establish a large-scale production line. Tianzhikefeng's technology has shown promising results, with its sulfide solid-state electrolyte materials demonstrating high ionic conductivity and air stability. The company aims to become a leading supplier of solid-state electrolyte materials for the battery industry.

## 02 Critical Resources Widens Solid-State Battery Push with NTU Thermal Management Licence
Critical Resources, an Australian company, has expanded its solid-state battery capabilities by acquiring a thermal management licence from Nanyang Technological University (NTU). The licence agreement will enable Critical Resources to develop more efficient and reliable solid-state batteries, addressing one of the key challenges in the technology's commercialization. The company's move into solid-state batteries is seen as a strategic diversification of its business, with the potential to create new revenue streams and drive growth.

## 03 A California Company Kicks Off Solid-State Battery Pilot Production
A California-based company has begun pilot production of solid-state batteries, marking a significant milestone in the technology's development. The company's solid-state batteries are designed to offer improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. While the company's name has not been disclosed, the news is seen as a positive development for the solid-state battery industry, which is expected to play a crucial role in the transition to electric vehicles and renewable energy systems.
