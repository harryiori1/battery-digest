ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery company, has partnered with Delta to develop a new battery energy management system for next-generation battery applications. The partnership will focus on designing and developing a highly advanced battery management system that will optimize the charging and discharging of ProLogium's batteries, leading to improved safety, reliability, and overall system efficiency.

According to ProLogium, this partnership will play a crucial role in the company's mission to commercialize its solid-state battery technology. The new battery energy management system will be designed to work seamlessly with ProLogium's solid-state batteries, enabling the widespread adoption of electric vehicles and other applications that require high-performance and reliable battery systems.

The partnership between ProLogium and Delta is a significant development in the solid-state battery industry, as it brings together two leading companies with expertise in battery technology and energy management systems. This collaboration is expected to accelerate the development of ProLogium's solid-state battery technology and pave the way for its commercialization in the near future.
