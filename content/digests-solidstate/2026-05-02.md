## 01 ProLogium Wins Edison Award for Third Time
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery company, has won the prestigious Edison Award for the third time. The award recognizes the company's next-generation solid-state battery technology, which has earned global recognition for its innovative approach to battery design and manufacturing. According to [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-wins-edison-award-for-the-third-time-next-generation-solid-state-battery-technology-earns-global-recognition-again/), this award is a testament to the company's commitment to developing cutting-edge battery technology that can meet the growing demand for electric vehicles and renewable energy systems.
