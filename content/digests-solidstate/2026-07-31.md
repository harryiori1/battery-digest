## 01 QuantumScape Achieves Eagle Line Pilot Production Success
QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery company, has announced the successful pilot production of its Eagle Line, a significant milestone in its pursuit of commercializing solid-state batteries. This achievement demonstrates the company's ability to scale up its production process, a crucial step towards mass production. The Eagle Line pilot production line is expected to produce cells with higher energy density and faster charging times, making them more competitive in the electric vehicle market. According to [QuantumScape's Q2 earnings report](https://investors.quantumscape.com/news/news-details/2026/QuantumScape-Announces-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx), the company has made significant progress in reducing its cash burn and narrowing its losses, which could reshape its licensing-led narrative.

## 02 Ilika Shows Commercial Progress in Stereax and Goliath Solid-State Battery Businesses
Ilika, a UK-based solid-state battery company, has announced commercial progress in its Stereax and Goliath businesses. The company has made significant advancements in its solid-state battery technology, which has the potential to disrupt the electric vehicle industry. Ilika's Stereax batteries are designed for small devices, such as wearables and medical implants, while its Goliath batteries are targeted at the electric vehicle market. According to [Ilika's press release](https://www.ilika.com/news/ilika-shows-commercial-progress-in-stereax-and-goliath-solid-state-battery-businesses/), the company has seen increased demand for its batteries, driven by the growing need for more efficient and sustainable energy storage solutions.

## 03 A Solid-State Battery Ran for a Year Underground in Beijing, Raising Questions About Its Potential in EVs
A recent experiment in Beijing has demonstrated the potential of solid-state batteries to power devices underground for extended periods. The battery, which was developed by a Chinese company, ran for a year without any significant degradation, raising questions about its potential use in electric vehicles. While solid-state batteries have shown promise in laboratory settings, their scalability and cost remain significant challenges. According to [CarNewsChina](https://www.carnewschina.com/a-solid-state-battery-ran-for-a-year-underground-in-beijing-why-isnt-it-in-evs-yet/), the experiment highlights the need for further research and development to overcome these challenges and make solid-state batteries a viable option for the electric vehicle industry.
