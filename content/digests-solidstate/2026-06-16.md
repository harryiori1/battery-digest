## 01 Solid-State Batteries Now Powering EVs in the Real World
Solid-state EV batteries are now being tested on the road in North America for the first time, promising longer range, faster charging, and lower costs. This development marks a significant milestone in the commercialization of solid-state battery technology. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/06/11/solid-state-batteries-now-powering-evs-in-real-world/), the testing of solid-state batteries in real-world conditions is a crucial step towards their widespread adoption. The success of these tests could pave the way for the mass production of solid-state batteries, which are expected to play a key role in the transition to electric vehicles.

## 02 ProLogium’s Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Integrated into Nakayama Iron Works’ Electric Self-Propelled Soil Improvement Machine
ProLogium's solid-state battery technology is being used in a new electric self-propelled soil improvement machine, demonstrating its potential for commercial applications. As reported by [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologiums-next-generation-solid-state-battery-integrated-into-nakayama-iron-works-ne100mg-electric-self-propelled-soil-improvement-machine/), the integration of their solid-state battery into the machine has enabled it to operate more efficiently and effectively. This collaboration highlights the versatility of solid-state battery technology and its potential to be used in a wide range of applications beyond electric vehicles.
