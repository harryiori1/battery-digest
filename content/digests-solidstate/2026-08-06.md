## 01 ProLogium to List on the Nasdaq through a Merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp.
ProLogium, a next-generation solid-state battery developer, has announced a merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp. to list on the Nasdaq. This move marks a significant step forward in the company's commercialization efforts, providing access to additional capital and resources to accelerate the development and deployment of its solid-state battery technology. With over 10 years of proven commercialization experience, ProLogium is well-positioned to capitalize on the growing demand for solid-state batteries in the electric vehicle and renewable energy sectors. As the company prepares to list on the Nasdaq, it is expected to attract increased attention from investors and industry partners, further solidifying its position as a leader in the solid-state battery market.

## 02 BYD Files Six Solid-State Battery Patents, Eyes 2027 Production
BYD has filed six patents for solid-state battery technology, with plans to begin production in 2027. The company's dual-electrolyte cathode cell design is expected to significantly improve the performance and safety of its solid-state batteries, making them more competitive in the market. BYD's investment in solid-state battery technology demonstrates its commitment to innovation and its desire to stay ahead of the curve in the rapidly evolving electric vehicle and renewable energy industries. As one of the largest battery manufacturers in the world, BYD's entry into the solid-state battery market is expected to have a significant impact on the industry, driving growth and adoption of this emerging technology.

## 03 Blue Solutions and AVL Launch Joint Safety Program for Next-Generation Solid-State Cell Technology
Blue Solutions and AVL have launched a joint safety program to prepare for the scale-up of next-generation solid-state cell technology. The program aims to ensure the safe development and deployment of this breakthrough technology, which has the potential to significantly improve the performance and efficiency of electric vehicles and renewable energy systems. By prioritizing safety, Blue Solutions and AVL are demonstrating their commitment to responsible innovation and their dedication to delivering high-quality products that meet the highest standards of safety and reliability. As the solid-state battery market continues to grow and evolve, the importance of safety will only continue to increase, making this joint safety program a critical step forward for the industry.
