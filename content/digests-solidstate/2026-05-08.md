## 01 Semi-Solid State Batteries Enter E-Bike Market
Semi-solid state batteries are finally making their way into the e-bike industry, as reported by Electrek. This development marks a significant shift towards more efficient and safer battery technology. Unlike traditional lithium-ion batteries, semi-solid state batteries replace the liquid electrolyte with a solid or semi-solid material, enhancing the overall performance and reducing the risk of thermal runaway. Companies are now integrating these batteries into e-bikes, offering improved range, charging speed, and durability. As the e-bike market continues to grow, the adoption of semi-solid state batteries is expected to increase, driving innovation and competition in the industry.

## 02 Pure Lithium New Energy Completes Pre-A+ Round Financing for All-Solid-State Batteries
Pure Lithium New Energy, a Chinese company, has announced the completion of its Pre-A+ round financing, securing tens of millions of yuan to support the development and commercialization of all-solid-state batteries. Founded in 2022, the company has made significant progress in breaking down the core issues of solid-state batteries, such as the solid-solid interface problem. With the new funding, Pure Lithium New Energy aims to scale up production and expand its product offerings, focusing on applications in the energy storage and electric vehicle sectors. The company's advancements in all-solid-state battery technology have the potential to revolutionize the industry, enabling the widespread adoption of safer, more efficient, and higher-energy-density batteries.

## 03 Blue Current Highlights $81M Series D and Solid-State Battery Milestones
Blue Current has announced the completion of its $81M Series D funding round, alongside significant milestones in its solid-state battery development. The company's progress in this field demonstrates the growing interest and investment in solid-state battery technology. With the new funding, Blue Current plans to further accelerate its research and development efforts, focusing on improving the performance, scalability, and cost-effectiveness of its solid-state batteries. As the industry continues to evolve, Blue Current's advancements are expected to play a crucial role in enabling the widespread adoption of solid-state batteries in various applications, from electric vehicles to renewable energy systems.
