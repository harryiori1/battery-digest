## 03 Helios Horizon Advances Electric Propulsion with Solid-State Battery Test Flights
Helios Horizon has successfully conducted test flights of its electric propulsion system, powered by solid-state batteries. This achievement marks a significant milestone in the development of sustainable aviation technology, as solid-state batteries offer improved safety, energy density, and charging speed compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. The use of solid-state batteries in electric propulsion systems has the potential to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and operating costs, making it an attractive option for the aviation industry. Helios Horizon's test flights demonstrate the feasibility of solid-state battery technology in electric propulsion systems, paving the way for further innovation and adoption in the aviation sector.
