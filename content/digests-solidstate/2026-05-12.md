## 03 SAIC Opens Pre-Orders for MG 4X with Semi-Solid-State Battery Option
SAIC has begun taking pre-orders for the MG 4X, an electric SUV that will offer a semi-solid-state battery option. This marks one of the first commercial applications of semi-solid-state battery technology in a production vehicle. The MG 4X is significant not only because it offers a semi-solid-state battery but also due to its potential to bring this advanced technology to a wider market. As more automotive manufacturers explore solid-state and semi-solid-state batteries, the industry is moving closer to achieving the high energy density, safety, and charging speeds that these technologies promise. SAIC's move is a step towards making semi-solid-state batteries more mainstream and could pave the way for wider adoption of solid-state battery technology in the future.
