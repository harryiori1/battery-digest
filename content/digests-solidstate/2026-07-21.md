## 01 ProLogium Signs MoU with Elysian Aircraft to Explore Solid-State Battery Applications in Aerospace
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery company, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Elysian Aircraft BV to explore the application of solid-state battery technology in aerospace. The partnership aims to advance the development of zero-emission aviation and future mobility. According to [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-and-elysian-aircraft-bv-sign-mou%ef%bc%9aexploring-next-generation-battery-applications-in-aerospace-to-advance-the-vision-of-zero-emission-aviation-and-future-mobility/), the collaboration will focus on designing and developing next-generation battery systems for electric and hybrid-electric aircraft.

This partnership is significant for the solid-state battery industry, as it marks a crucial step towards the commercialization of this technology in the aerospace sector. Solid-state batteries offer several advantages over traditional lithium-ion batteries, including improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds. The successful integration of solid-state batteries in aircraft could pave the way for the widespread adoption of electric and hybrid-electric propulsion systems in the aviation industry.

## 02 ProLogium Partners with OPmobility to Develop Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Modules
ProLogium has also announced a partnership with OPmobility to develop next-generation solid-state battery modules for mobility applications. The collaboration aims to create advanced battery modules that can be used in a wide range of electric vehicles, from passenger cars to commercial trucks. As reported by [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-and-opmobility-sign-mou-to-develop-next-generation-solid-state-battery-modules-for-mobility/), the partnership will focus on designing and developing battery modules that meet the highest standards of safety, performance, and reliability.

This partnership is a significant development for the solid-state battery industry, as it demonstrates the growing interest in this technology among mobility companies. The successful development of next-generation solid-state battery modules could enable the widespread adoption of electric vehicles, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and improving air quality in urban areas.

## 03 Blue Solutions Launches Joint Safety Program with AVL to Prepare for Solid-State Cell Technology Scale-Up
Blue Solutions, a leading solid-state battery company, has launched a joint safety program with AVL, a renowned automotive engineering company, to prepare for the scale-up of solid-state cell technology. The program aims to ensure the safe and efficient production of next-generation solid-state batteries, which are expected to play a crucial role in the transition to electric mobility. According to [Blue Solutions](https://www.blue-solutions.com/blue-solutions-and-avl-launch-complete-joint-safety-program-to-prepare-next-generation-solid-state-cell-technology-scale-up/), the program will focus on developing and implementing safety protocols, testing procedures, and quality control measures to ensure the highest standards of safety and performance.

This program is a significant development for the solid-state battery industry, as it demonstrates the growing recognition of the need for safety and quality control in the production of next-generation batteries. The successful implementation of this program could enable the widespread adoption of solid-state batteries in electric vehicles, reducing the risk of accidents and improving overall safety.
