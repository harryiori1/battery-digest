## 01 ProLogium’s Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Integrated into Nakayama Iron Works’ NE100MG Electric Self-Propelled Soil Improvement Machine
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery manufacturer, has announced the integration of its next-generation solid-state battery into Nakayama Iron Works’ NE100MG electric self-propelled soil improvement machine. This marks a significant milestone in the commercialization of solid-state battery technology, as it demonstrates the feasibility of using solid-state batteries in heavy-duty industrial applications. The NE100MG machine is designed for soil improvement and compaction, and the integration of ProLogium’s solid-state battery is expected to improve its performance, efficiency, and safety. This development is a major step forward for the adoption of solid-state batteries in the industrial sector, and it highlights ProLogium’s commitment to pushing the boundaries of this technology.

## 02 BMW Group and Solid Power Take Next Step in ASSB Development Path with New Partner Samsung SDI
BMW Group and Solid Power have announced a new partnership with Samsung SDI to develop all-solid-state batteries. This partnership is a significant step forward in the development of solid-state battery technology, as it brings together three major players in the industry. The partnership aims to accelerate the commercialization of all-solid-state batteries, which are expected to offer improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. Samsung SDI will contribute its expertise in battery cell production, while BMW Group and Solid Power will provide their knowledge of solid-state battery technology and automotive applications. This collaboration is expected to drive innovation and push the boundaries of solid-state battery technology.
