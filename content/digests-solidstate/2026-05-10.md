## 01 Pure Lithium New Energy Completes Pre-A+ Round Financing for All-Solid-State Batteries
Pure Lithium New Energy, a Beijing-based company, has completed a Pre-A+ round financing, securing tens of millions of yuan to further develop its all-solid-state battery technology. The funding will be used to support product research and development, market expansion, and production line construction. Pure Lithium New Energy was founded in 2022 and has been focused on developing all-solid-state batteries using its proprietary composite electrolyte and supercritical coating technology. The company has already achieved significant milestones, including the construction of a small-scale production line in Beijing and a 500MWh production line in Henan. With this new funding, Pure Lithium New Energy is well-positioned to continue its growth and development in the solid-state battery market.

## 02 ProLogium Wins Edison Award for Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Technology
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery company, has been awarded the Edison Award for the third time, recognizing its innovative solid-state battery technology and its potential to revolutionize the industry. The Edison Award is a prestigious honor that acknowledges excellence in innovation and innovation leadership. ProLogium's solid-state battery technology has been recognized for its high energy density, long cycle life, and excellent safety performance. The company's technology has the potential to significantly improve the performance and range of electric vehicles, and its award-winning technology is a testament to its commitment to innovation and excellence.

## 03 QuantumScape Launches 'Eagle' SSB Production Line
QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery company, has launched its 'Eagle' solid-state battery production line, marking a significant milestone in the company's efforts to commercialize its solid-state battery technology. The 'Eagle' production line is designed to produce high-quality solid-state batteries with high energy density and long cycle life. The launch of the 'Eagle' production line is a major step forward for QuantumScape, as it demonstrates the company's ability to scale up its production capabilities and meet the growing demand for solid-state batteries.
