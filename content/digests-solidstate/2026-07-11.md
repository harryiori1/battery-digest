## MG to Launch First PHEV with Semi-Solid-State Batteries in 2027
MG is set to launch its first plug-in hybrid electric vehicle (PHEV) equipped with semi-solid-state batteries in 2027. The company plans to introduce this new battery technology in its upcoming plug-in hybrid SUVs, starting with the MG ZS PHEV. This move marks a significant step towards the adoption of solid-state battery technology in the automotive industry.

According to reports, MG's semi-solid-state batteries will not be exclusive to EVs, with plans to launch three new plug-in hybrid SUVs equipped with the new battery tech. The MG ZS PHEV is expected to be the first model to feature this technology, with a launch date set for 2027.

The use of semi-solid-state batteries is expected to improve the overall performance and efficiency of MG's PHEVs. Semi-solid-state batteries offer several advantages over traditional lithium-ion batteries, including improved safety, higher energy density, and faster charging times.

MG's decision to adopt semi-solid-state batteries is a significant development in the automotive industry, as it could pave the way for wider adoption of this technology in the future. As the industry continues to shift towards more sustainable and efficient energy solutions, the use of semi-solid-state batteries is likely to play a key role in this transition.

It is worth noting that while semi-solid-state batteries are not yet fully solid-state, they represent a significant step towards the development of fully solid-state batteries. Fully solid-state batteries have the potential to offer even greater improvements in safety, energy density, and charging times, and are widely seen as the next major breakthrough in battery technology.

As the automotive industry continues to evolve, it will be interesting to see how MG's adoption of semi-solid-state batteries impacts the market and influences other manufacturers to follow suit. With the launch of the MG ZS PHEV in 2027, we can expect to see more developments in this space and a greater focus on the adoption of solid-state battery technology in the years to come.
