## 01 Ganfeng Lithium Begins Production of World's First 10 Ah Solid-State Batteries
Ganfeng Lithium, a Chinese lithium metal giant, has begun producing the world's first 10 Ah solid-state batteries with an energy density of 500 Wh/kg. This major milestone in solid-state battery development is significant for the industry, as it brings the technology one step closer to commercialization. The batteries are expected to have a significant impact on the electric vehicle (EV) market, as they offer improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. Ganfeng Lithium has supply deals with major OEMs such as Tesla, Volkswagen, and Hyundai, which could potentially lead to the adoption of these solid-state batteries in their EV models. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/05/21/solid-state-ev-batteries-hit-another-major-milestone-china/), the production of these batteries marks a significant achievement in the development of solid-state battery technology.

The implications of this development are substantial, as solid-state batteries have the potential to revolutionize the EV industry. With their improved energy density and safety features, they could enable the production of EVs with longer ranges and faster charging times, making them more competitive with traditional internal combustion engine vehicles. Additionally, the use of solid-state batteries could reduce the risk of battery fires and improve the overall safety of EVs.

As the industry continues to develop and commercialize solid-state battery technology, we can expect to see significant advancements in the coming years. Companies like Ganfeng Lithium, ProLogium, and Ilika are at the forefront of this development, and their progress will be crucial in determining the future of the EV industry. With the production of the world's first 10 Ah solid-state batteries, Ganfeng Lithium has taken a major step forward in this journey, and it will be exciting to see how the industry evolves from here.
