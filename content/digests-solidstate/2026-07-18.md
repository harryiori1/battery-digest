QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery developer, has partnered with Honda to advance the development of solid-state batteries for electric vehicles. ## 02 Stellantis and Factorial Integration
Stellantis, a leading automotive manufacturer, has partnered with Factorial, a solid-state battery developer, to integrate advanced solid-state batteries into its development vehicles. As reported by [Stellantis Media](https://media.stellantis.com/em-en/press/article/stellantis-and-factorial-integrate-advanced-solid-state-battery-into-stellantis-development-vehicle-and-launch-road-testing), road testing has begun for the integrated solid-state battery, marking a major milestone in the technology's development. This partnership is expected to drive innovation and investment in the solid-state battery industry.

## 03 China's New Solid-State Law

The law is expected to support the development of solid-state batteries, which are seen as a key technology for the widespread adoption of electric vehicles.
