## 01 Solid-State EV Batteries Are Coming Sooner Than Expected
Greater Bay Technology has announced a breakthrough in solid-state battery technology, aiming to launch the world's first mass-producible all-solid-state battery this year. This development is significant for the electric vehicle (EV) industry, as solid-state batteries offer improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. With this innovation, Greater Bay Technology is poised to play a crucial role in the commercialization of solid-state batteries, which could accelerate the adoption of EVs worldwide. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/04/15/solid-state-ev-batteries-coming-sooner-than-expected/), the company's achievement is a major step forward in making solid-state batteries a reality for the mass market.

## 02 ProLogium Partners with Delta to Develop a New Battery Energy Management System
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery manufacturer, has partnered with Delta to develop a new battery energy management system (BEMS) for next-generation battery applications. This collaboration aims to enhance the performance and efficiency of solid-state batteries, which are critical for the widespread adoption of electric vehicles. The BEMS will be designed to optimize battery lifespan, charging speeds, and overall system reliability. As reported by [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-partners-with-delta-to-develop-a-new-battery-energy-management-system-for-next-generation-battery-applications/), this partnership will drive innovation in solid-state battery technology and contribute to the growth of the EV industry.

## 03 Blue Solutions Revolutionizes Electric Mobility with Groundbreaking GEN4 Battery Technology
Blue Solutions has introduced its GEN4 battery technology, which promises to revolutionize electric mobility with its groundbreaking solid-state battery innovations. The GEN4 battery is designed to provide enhanced energy density, safety, and charging speeds, making it an attractive solution for electric vehicle manufacturers. According to [Blue Solutions](https://www.blue-solutions.com/gen4-battery-how-blue-solutions-is-revolutionizing-electric-mobility-with-its-groundbreaking-technology/), the GEN4 battery technology has the potential to significantly improve the performance and range of electric vehicles, driving the adoption of sustainable transportation solutions.
