## SK On and Factorial Energy Partner to Scale Solid-State Battery Tech
SK On and Factorial Energy have announced a new alliance that will focus on bringing solid-state batteries to the masses. This partnership is a significant development in the solid-state battery industry, as it combines the expertise of two leading companies in the field. SK On, a major EV battery manufacturer, and Factorial Energy, a solid-state battery startup, will work together to scale up solid-state battery production and make it more affordable for the mass market. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/29/solid-state-ev-battery-leaders-team-up/), the partnership will enable the companies to leverage each other's strengths and accelerate the development of solid-state battery technology.

The partnership is expected to have a significant impact on the EV industry, as solid-state batteries have the potential to offer improved safety, range, and charging speeds compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. With this alliance, SK On and Factorial Energy are well-positioned to become leaders in the solid-state battery market and drive the adoption of EVs worldwide.

## ProLogium Announces $50 Million Committed Primary Capital Investment
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery developer, has announced a $50 million committed primary capital investment from Translational Development Acquisition Corp. This investment is a significant milestone for ProLogium, as it will support the company's proposed business combination and help accelerate the development of its solid-state battery technology. According to [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-and-translational-development-acquisition-corp-announce-50-million-committed-primary-capital-investment-to-support-proposed-business-combination/), the investment will enable the company to further enhance its research and development capabilities and expand its production capacity.

The investment is a testament to ProLogium's progress in developing its solid-state battery technology, which has the potential to revolutionize the EV industry. With this funding, ProLogium is well-positioned to become a leading player in the solid-state battery market and drive the adoption of EVs worldwide.

## Blue Solutions and AVL Launch Joint Safety Program for Solid-State Cell Technology
Blue Solutions, a leading solid-state battery developer, has partnered with AVL, a renowned automotive technology company, to launch a joint safety program for solid-state cell technology. The program aims to prepare next-generation solid-state cell technology for scale up and ensure its safe deployment in the EV industry. According to [Blue Solutions](https://www.blue-solutions.com/blue-solutions-and-avl-launch-complete-joint-safety-program-to-prepare-next-generation-solid-state-cell-technology-scale-up/), the partnership will enable the companies to leverage each other's expertise and develop a comprehensive safety program for solid-state batteries.

The joint safety program is a significant development in the solid-state battery industry, as it addresses one of the key challenges facing the technology: safety. By developing a comprehensive safety program, Blue Solutions and AVL are helping to mitigate the risks associated with solid-state batteries and pave the way for their widespread adoption in the EV industry.
