## 01 ProLogium to Go Public Through Merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp.
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery developer, has announced its plan to list on the Nasdaq through a merger with Translational Development Acquisition Corp., a special purpose acquisition company (SPAC). This move marks a significant milestone in the company's history, as it seeks to accelerate its growth and commercialization of solid-state battery technology. With over 10 years of experience in developing and commercializing solid-state batteries, ProLogium is well-positioned to capitalize on the growing demand for more efficient and sustainable energy storage solutions. The merger is expected to provide ProLogium with the necessary funding to expand its operations and further develop its technology.

SAIC has launched a new electric SUV in China, featuring a semi-solid-state EV battery. SAIC's decision to adopt this technology demonstrates the company's commitment to innovation and its efforts to stay ahead of the curve in the rapidly evolving electric vehicle market.

## 03 Argonne Scientists Discover Method to Boost Solid-State Battery Energy Density and Longevity
Researchers at Argonne National Laboratory have made a breakthrough in solid-state battery technology, discovering a way to increase energy density and longevity. The team's findings, published in a recent study, reveal that by using a specific combination of materials, solid-state batteries can achieve higher energy densities and longer lifetimes. This discovery has significant implications for the development of more efficient and sustainable energy storage solutions, and could pave the way for the widespread adoption of solid-state batteries in a range of applications, from electric vehicles to renewable energy systems.
