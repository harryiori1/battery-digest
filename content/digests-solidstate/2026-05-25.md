## 01 Ganfeng Lithium Begins Production of World's First 10 Ah Solid-State Batteries
Ganfeng Lithium, a Chinese lithium metal giant, has begun producing the world's first 10 Ah solid-state batteries with an energy density of 500 Wh/kg. This major milestone in solid-state battery development is a significant step towards commercialization. The company has supply deals with major OEMs such as Tesla, Volkswagen, and Hyundai for key EV battery materials. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/05/21/solid-state-ev-batteries-hit-another-major-milestone-china/), the production of these solid-state batteries marks a crucial achievement in the industry's pursuit of safer, more efficient, and higher-energy-density batteries. This development has significant implications for the future of electric vehicles and renewable energy storage, as solid-state batteries have the potential to replace traditional lithium-ion batteries with improved performance and reduced risk of thermal runaway. As the industry continues to push the boundaries of solid-state battery technology, companies like Ganfeng Lithium are at the forefront of innovation, driving the transition towards a more sustainable and electrified future.
