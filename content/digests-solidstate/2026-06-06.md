## 01 ProLogium Breaks Ground on Dunkirk Gigafactory in France
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery manufacturer, has begun construction on its new gigafactory in Dunkirk, France. This significant milestone marks the company's commitment to expanding its production capacity and meeting the growing demand for solid-state batteries. The Dunkirk gigafactory is expected to play a crucial role in ProLogium's plans to scale up its solid-state battery production, with the company aiming to produce high-performance, safe, and reliable batteries for the electric vehicle (EV) and energy storage markets. As the company continues to advance its solid-state battery technology, the Dunkirk gigafactory will be a key facility in ProLogium's global production network.

## 02 Blue Solutions and AVL Launch Joint Safety Program for Solid-State Cell Technology
Blue Solutions, a pioneer in solid-state battery technology, has partnered with AVL, a leading automotive technology company, to launch a comprehensive joint safety program. The program aims to prepare the next generation of solid-state cell technology for scale-up, ensuring the safe and reliable production of high-performance solid-state batteries. The partnership brings together Blue Solutions' expertise in solid-state battery technology and AVL's experience in automotive safety and testing, creating a robust safety framework for the development and production of solid-state batteries. This collaboration is a significant step forward in the commercialization of solid-state battery technology, as it addresses the critical issue of safety and reliability in the production of these advanced batteries.

## 03 QuantumScape CTO Sells 150,320 Shares, Company Blueprint for Future of Energy Storage
QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery developer, has announced that its CTO, Timothy Holme, has sold 150,320 shares. Meanwhile, the company has released its blueprint for the future of energy storage, highlighting its commitment to solid-state battery technology. The blueprint outlines QuantumScape's vision for the development and commercialization of solid-state batteries, which the company believes will play a crucial role in the transition to a sustainable energy future. With its solid-state battery technology, QuantumScape aims to enable the widespread adoption of electric vehicles and renewable energy systems, reducing greenhouse gas emissions and mitigating climate change. The company's blueprint provides a roadmap for the development of its solid-state battery technology, including plans for scaling up production and expanding its manufacturing capabilities.
