## 01 QuantumScape and Honda Partner on Solid-State Battery Research
QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery developer, has partnered with Honda to advance the research and development of solid-state batteries. This partnership is a significant development in the solid-state battery space, as two industry leaders join forces to accelerate the commercialization of this technology. According to [QuantumScape's website](https://www.quantumscape.com/), the company's solid-state batteries have the potential to significantly improve the range and efficiency of electric vehicles. With Honda's expertise in automotive manufacturing and QuantumScape's innovative battery technology, this partnership is expected to drive the adoption of solid-state batteries in the automotive industry.

## 02 New Ceramic Solid-State Battery Survives Extreme Heat
A new ceramic solid-state battery has been developed, which can survive extreme heat of up to 302°F. This breakthrough in solid-state battery technology has potential applications in military and aerospace, where high-temperature resistance is critical. According to [Interesting Engineering](https://interestingengineering.com/), the new battery uses a ceramic material that is more stable and durable than traditional battery materials. This development is significant, as it demonstrates the potential of solid-state batteries to operate in extreme environments.

## 03 Mass Commissioning of 100-mt-Class All-Solid-State Battery Production Lines
The mass commissioning of 100-mt-class all-solid-state battery production lines is expected to become an industry consensus by 2027.
