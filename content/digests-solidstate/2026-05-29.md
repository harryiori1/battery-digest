## 01 Ganfeng Lithium Begins Producing World's First 10 Ah Solid-State Batteries
Ganfeng Lithium, a Chinese lithium metal giant, has begun producing the world's first 10 Ah solid-state batteries with an energy density of 500 Wh/kg. This major milestone in solid-state battery production is a significant step forward for the industry. The company has supply deals with major OEMs such as Tesla, Volkswagen, and Hyundai, and this development is expected to have a significant impact on the electric vehicle market. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/05/21/solid-state-ev-batteries-hit-another-major-milestone-china/), Ganfeng Lithium's solid-state batteries have the potential to revolutionize the EV industry with their high energy density and improved safety features.

## 02 Blue Solutions Launches Joint Safety Program for Next-Generation Solid-State Cell Technology
Blue Solutions has launched a joint safety program with AVL to prepare for the scale-up of next-generation solid-state cell technology. The program aims to ensure the safe development and production of solid-state batteries, which are expected to play a crucial role in the transition to electric vehicles. As reported by [Blue Solutions](https://www.blue-solutions.com/blue-solutions-and-avl-launch-complete-joint-safety-program-to-prepare-next-generation-solid-state-cell-technology-scale-up/), the partnership will focus on developing safety standards and protocols for solid-state battery production.

## 03 ProLogium Breaks Ground on Dunkirk Gigafactory in France
ProLogium has broken ground on its Dunkirk gigafactory in France, marking a major step forward for the company's solid-state battery production plans. The gigafactory is expected to produce high-performance solid-state batteries for the electric vehicle market. According to [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-breaks-ground-on-dunkirk-gigafactory-in-france/), the Dunkirk gigafactory will be a key component of the company's strategy to become a leading player in the solid-state battery market.
