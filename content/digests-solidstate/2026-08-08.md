## 02 Electrical Imbalances at Grain Boundaries Help Explain Solid-State Battery Failure
Researchers have made a breakthrough in understanding the challenges facing solid-state battery development. According to [Phys.org](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMihgFBVV95cUxONlhyWTExQURJWjdIa1ZHTm1jVG9CMWZCeXhqVkhDUnJ2Nk8zWVYwODJIWnZkS09jYnJHZi0xQ1J5MGs0cHotREtOU3lrUTFfLWRXb2hsSkV4dHBmY3JETWVQQWcyYXZHdlR1MHdpQkZ0ODlXYnk5cWNrUEZZYmw3ZGxLYkFRZw), electrical imbalances at grain boundaries are a key factor in solid-state battery failure. This discovery could lead to the development of more reliable and efficient solid-state batteries. The research team used advanced characterization techniques to study the grain boundaries in solid-state battery materials and found that electrical imbalances can cause battery failure.
