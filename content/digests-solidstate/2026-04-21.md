## 01 Ningde Era Discusses Solid-State Battery Cooperation with Chinese High-End Car Companies
Ningde Era, a leading Chinese battery manufacturer, is in talks with several Chinese high-end car companies to discuss solid-state battery cooperation. According to [36氪](https://36kr.com/newsflashes/3777324535597320), the company's chief technology officer, Gao Huan, stated that the sample development progress of solid-state batteries is going smoothly. This cooperation is a significant step towards the commercialization of solid-state batteries in the Chinese automotive market. With solid-state batteries offering improved safety, energy density, and charging speeds, they are expected to play a crucial role in the development of future electric vehicles.

The Verge TS Pro Gen2 is a testament to the potential of solid-state batteries in the development of future electric vehicles.
