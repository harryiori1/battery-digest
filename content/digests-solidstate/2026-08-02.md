## 01 SK On and Factorial Energy Team Up to Scale Solid-State Battery Tech
SK On and Factorial Energy have announced a new alliance to bring solid-state batteries to the masses. This collaboration aims to scale up the production of solid-state batteries, which are considered a breakthrough technology in the electric vehicle (EV) industry. The partnership will focus on developing and commercializing solid-state batteries, with the goal of making them more widely available for EV manufacturers. This move is significant, as it could help accelerate the adoption of solid-state batteries in the EV market. According to [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/07/29/solid-state-ev-battery-leaders-team-up/), the two companies will work together to develop and manufacture solid-state batteries, with SK On providing its expertise in battery production and Factorial Energy contributing its solid-state battery technology.

## 02 ProLogium to Develop Next-Generation Solid-State Battery Modules for Mobility
ProLogium has signed a memorandum of understanding (MoU) with OPmobility to develop next-generation solid-state battery modules for mobility applications. This partnership aims to create high-performance, solid-state battery modules that can be used in a variety of electric vehicles, including cars, buses, and trucks. ProLogium's solid-state battery technology is considered one of the most advanced in the industry, with high energy density and fast charging capabilities. The company's partnership with OPmobility will help to accelerate the development and commercialization of its solid-state battery modules. As reported by [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-and-opmobility-sign-mou-to-develop-next-generation-solid-state-battery-modules-for-mobility/), the two companies will work together to design, develop, and manufacture solid-state battery modules that meet the needs of the mobility industry.

## 03 Blue Solutions Demonstrates 70% More Autonomy with Breakthrough GEN4 Lithium-Metal Batteries
Blue Solutions has announced a significant breakthrough in its GEN4 lithium-metal battery technology, demonstrating 70% more autonomy than previous generations. The company's GEN4 batteries use a unique lithium-metal design that provides higher energy density and faster charging capabilities than traditional lithium-ion batteries. Blue Solutions' breakthrough has significant implications for the electric vehicle industry, as it could enable the development of EVs with longer ranges and faster charging times. According to [Blue Solutions](https://www.blue-solutions.com/blue-solutions-demonstrates-70-more-autonomy-with-its-breakthrough-gen4-lithium-metal-batteries/), the company's GEN4 batteries have been tested in a variety of applications, including electric vehicles and renewable energy systems, and have shown significant improvements in performance and efficiency.
