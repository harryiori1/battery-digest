## 01 KERI Resolves Interfacial Instability in All-Solid-State Battery Commercialization
KERI, a research institution, has made a significant breakthrough in all-solid-state battery commercialization by resolving the issue of interfacial instability. This development is crucial for the widespread adoption of solid-state batteries, as it addresses one of the major challenges hindering their commercialization. Interfacial instability refers to the degradation of the interface between the solid electrolyte and the electrode, which can lead to a decrease in battery performance and lifespan. By resolving this issue, KERI has paved the way for the development of more efficient and reliable solid-state batteries. According to [EurekAlert!](https://www.eurekalert.org/), this breakthrough has the potential to significantly improve the performance and safety of solid-state batteries.

## 02 QuantumScape Eagle Line Ramps Up as New Sectors Test Licensing Ambitions
QuantumScape, a leading solid-state battery technology company, has announced that its Eagle Line is ramping up production as new sectors test its licensing ambitions. ## 03 Verge Motorcycles Makes History with First Motorcycles Featuring Solid-State Battery Technology
Verge Motorcycles has made history by becoming the first company to produce motorcycles featuring solid-state battery technology. This development marks a significant milestone in the adoption of solid-state batteries in the transportation sector. As reported by [Verge Motorcycles](https://vergemotorcycles.com/), the use of solid-state batteries in motorcycles offers several advantages, including improved safety, increased range, and reduced charging times. This breakthrough has the potential to revolutionize the motorcycle industry and pave the way for the widespread adoption of solid-state batteries in other transportation sectors.
