## 02 BMW Group and Solid Power Take Next Step in ASSB Development Path with New Partner Samsung SDI
BMW Group and Solid Power have partnered with Samsung SDI to advance the development of all-solid-state batteries (ASSBs). This collaboration marks a significant step towards commercializing ASSBs, which are considered a crucial technology for the future of electric vehicles. The partnership will enable the development of high-performance ASSBs that can meet the demands of the automotive industry. Samsung SDI will bring its expertise in battery cell production to the table, while Solid Power will contribute its knowledge of solid-state battery technology. The collaboration is expected to accelerate the development of ASSBs and pave the way for their widespread adoption in the automotive industry. The partnership is a testament to the growing interest in solid-state battery technology and the importance of collaboration in driving innovation.
