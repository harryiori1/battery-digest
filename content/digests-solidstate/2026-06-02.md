## 01 BYD Plans to Bring All-Solid-State Batteries to EVs by 2027
BYD, a leading electric vehicle manufacturer, plans to bring all-solid-state batteries to its EVs by 2027, according to a recent report by [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/06/01/byd-all-solid-state-batteries-evs-by-2027/). This move is expected to significantly improve the performance and safety of BYD's EVs. All-solid-state batteries have several advantages over traditional lithium-ion batteries, including higher energy density, faster charging times, and improved safety. BYD's decision to adopt all-solid-state batteries is a significant step towards the commercialization of this technology.
