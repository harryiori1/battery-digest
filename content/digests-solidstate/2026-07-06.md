## 01 QuantumScape and Honda Partner to Develop Solid-State Batteries
QuantumScape, a leading developer of solid-state battery technology, has announced a partnership with Honda to advance the development of solid-state batteries. This collaboration aims to accelerate the commercialization of solid-state batteries, which are expected to significantly improve the performance, safety, and charging speed of electric vehicles. The partnership is seen as a major step forward in the development of solid-state battery technology and is expected to have a significant impact on the electric vehicle industry.
