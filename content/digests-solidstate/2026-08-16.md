## 01 ProLogium Secures $50 Million Investment to Support Solid-State Battery Development
ProLogium, a leading solid-state battery developer, has announced a $50 million committed primary capital investment to support its proposed business combination with Translational Development Acquisition Corp. This investment will help ProLogium advance its solid-state battery technology, which has the potential to significantly improve the safety, energy density, and charging speed of electric vehicles. According to [ProLogium](https://prologium.com/prologium-and-translational-development-acquisition-corp-announce-50-million-committed-primary-capital-investment-to-support-proposed-business-combination/), this investment will enable the company to further develop its technology and bring it to market.

## 02 Blue Solutions Launches Joint Safety Program for Next-Generation Solid-State Cell Technology
Blue Solutions, a company specializing in solid-state battery technology, has launched a joint safety program with AVL to ensure the safe scale-up of its next-generation solid-state cell technology. This program aims to develop a comprehensive safety framework for the production and use of solid-state batteries, which is critical for their widespread adoption in electric vehicles. As reported by [Blue Solutions](https://www.blue-solutions.com/blue-solutions-and-avl-launch-complete-joint-safety-program-to-prepare-next-generation-solid-state-cell-technology-scale-up/), this partnership will help to mitigate the risks associated with solid-state battery technology and accelerate its commercialization.

## 03 CATL Announces 500 Wh/kg Solid-State Battery with Sulfide Patent and 2027 Pilot Production Plans
CATL, a leading battery manufacturer, has announced the development of a 500 Wh/kg solid-state battery with a sulfide electrolyte. This battery has the potential to significantly improve the energy density and safety of electric vehicles.
