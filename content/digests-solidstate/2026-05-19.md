## 01 Svolt to Mass-Produce Hybrid Solid-Liquid Batteries by September
Svolt, a leading battery manufacturer, has announced plans to mass-produce hybrid solid-liquid batteries by September. This new technology aims to achieve liquid battery costs while maintaining the benefits of solid-state batteries. According to [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/05/19/svolt-plans-mass-produce-hybrid-solid-liquid-batteries-september-liquid-battery-costs/), Svolt's hybrid solid-liquid batteries will be produced at a cost comparable to traditional liquid batteries, making them a competitive option for the market.
