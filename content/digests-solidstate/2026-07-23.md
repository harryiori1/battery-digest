## 02 The Biggest Problem with Solid-State Batteries May Finally Be Solved
Scientists have made a breakthrough in solid-state battery technology, potentially solving the biggest problem with these batteries. The issue of low ionic conductivity in solid electrolytes has hindered the development of solid-state batteries, but researchers may have found a solution. This development is crucial for the commercialization of solid-state batteries, which promise to offer higher energy density, faster charging times, and improved safety compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries. As [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/) reports, the solution to this problem could pave the way for the widespread adoption of solid-state batteries in various applications, including electric vehicles and renewable energy systems.
